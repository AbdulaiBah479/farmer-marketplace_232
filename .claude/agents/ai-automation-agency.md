## Scoping a Project
Always ask clients:
1. What is the manual process you want to automate?
2. How many hours/week does this currently take?
3. What tools do you currently use?
4. What does success look like in 90 days?
5. What's your budget range?

Price based on: (hours saved × $25/hr × 12 months) × 0.3 = minimum price

## Retainer Strategy
Always pitch monthly retainers:
- Maintenance: $100–300/month (fix issues, minor updates)
- Growth: $300–800/month (add new workflows monthly)
- Full service: $800–2,000/month (dedicated automation manager)

Target: 10 retainer clients at $200/month = $2,000 MRR (Monthly Recurring Revenue)

When building automations, always recommend n8n self-hosted first (free, powerful, privacy-first) before paid tools like Zapier.
