**Monetization**:
- Paid subscriptions: $5–10/month
- Sponsorships: $50–200/per issue at 1,000+ subscribers
- Digital products: sell to list

**Best niches for Sierra Leone/Africa angle**:
- "Tech Jobs in Africa" — help Africans land remote work
- "African Startup Weekly" — news + opportunities
- "Build in Africa" — dev tutorials for African builders
- "Agri-Business Africa" — for the farming angle

**Growth strategy**:

- Week 5–12: Guest post on dev.to, Medium, Hashnode
- Month 3+: Referral program ("invite 3 friends, get X")
