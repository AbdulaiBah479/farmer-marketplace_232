## Email Sequences That Convert

### The Welcome Sequence (5 emails, set and forget)
```
Email 1 (Immediate): Deliver the lead magnet + welcome
Subject: "Here's your [lead magnet name], [First name]"
Body:
- Lead magnet download link
- 1 sentence about who you are
- Tell them what emails they'll receive
- 1 call to action (reply with their #1 challenge)

Email 2 (Day 2): Your story / establish authority
Subject: "How I went from [struggle] to [result]"
Body:
- Relatable origin story (be vulnerable)
- The turning point / key insight
- Promise: "Over the next few days, I'll share exactly how"

Email 3 (Day 4): Pure value
Subject: "The [topic] mistake 90% of [audience] make"
Body:
- Teach one high-value concept
- Share a counterintuitive insight
- No pitch

Email 4 (Day 7): Case study
Subject: "[Name] made $[X] doing [Y] in [timeframe]"
Body:
- Client/personal success story
- Specific, tangible results
- Soft transition: "Here's how I can help you do the same..."
- Introduce your product/service

Email 5 (Day 10): The offer
Subject: "Ready to [achieve goal]? Here's how I can help"
Body:
- Acknowledge they've been on the list for 10 days
- Summarize the value you've delivered
- Clear offer: product/service/consultation
- Specific CTA: "Click here to book a free strategy call"
```

### Abandoned Cart Sequence (E-Commerce)
```
Email 1 (1 hour): Gentle reminder
Subject: "You left something behind..."
Body:
- Product image
- "Did something come up? Your [product] is still available."
- Direct link back to cart

Email 2 (24 hours): Address objections
Subject: "Still thinking about [product]?"
Body:
- Social proof: "2,847 customers love it"
- Answer top 3 objections
- Money-back guarantee reminder
- Direct cart link

Email 3 (72 hours): Scarcity + small discount
Subject: "Last chance — 10% off expires tonight"
Body:
- Urgency: limited-time discount
- 1 powerful testimonial
- Clear CTA

Recovery rate: 5–15% of abandoned carts → significant revenue
```

### Re-engagement Sequence (Clean Inactive Subscribers)
```
Email 1: "We miss you"
Subject: "Haven't heard from you, [First name]..."
Body: "You haven't opened an email in 90 days. Still interested in [topic]?"
CTA: "YES, keep me subscribed" button

Email 2 (5 days later): Best content
Subject: "Our most popular post this year (in case you missed it)"
Body: Share your highest-performing content of all time

Email 3 (10 days later): Final warning
Subject: "Removing you from the list in 48 hours..."
Body: Honest — "I clean my list regularly. If you want to stay, click here."
CTA: "YES! Keep me subscribed"

Unsubscribe non-openers: Improves deliverability and open rates
```

## Email Deliverability

### The Technical Setup (Do This First)
```
DNS Records Required:

1. SPF (Sender Policy Framework):
   v=spf1 include:sendgrid.net ~all
   
2. DKIM (DomainKeys Identified Mail):
   Your email provider gives you the record to add to DNS
   
3. DMARC (Domain-based Message Authentication):
   v=DMARC1; p=none; rua=mailto:you@domain.com
   
4. Custom sending domain:
   Instead of "via mailchimp.com", send from your own domain
   emails@yoursite.com → looks professional, improves deliverability

Check your setup:
- mail-tester.com (aim for 9+/10 score)
- MXToolbox.com for DNS health
```

### Deliverability Best Practices
```
DO:
✓ Clean list every 90 days (remove non-openers)
✓ Use double opt-in for new subscribers
✓ Send consistently (1–4x/week minimum)
✓ Segment by engagement level
✓ Monitor spam complaint rate (keep under 0.08%)

DON'T:
✗ Use all-caps or spam trigger words (FREE!!!, CLICK NOW, $$$)
✗ Buy email lists
✗ Send to unengaged subscribers for months
✗ Have more than 1 image per 3 lines of text
✗ Use URL shorteners (Bit.ly) — spam filters flag them
```
