## Grant Proposal Structure

### Executive Summary (1 page — make or break)
- Problem statement (1 sentence)
- Your solution (1 sentence)
- Evidence it works (1 number)
- Scale potential (1 number)
- Amount requested and use of funds

### Full Proposal Template
```
1. PROBLEM STATEMENT
   - Quantify the problem with data
   - Explain who is affected and how
   - Why current solutions fail

2. YOUR SOLUTION
   - What you've built/will build
   - How it works (simply)
   - Why your approach is unique

3. THEORY OF CHANGE
   Activities → Outputs → Outcomes → Impact
   "If we do X, then Y will happen, leading to Z impact"

4. EVIDENCE & TRACTION
   - Pilots completed, users served
   - Revenue if any
   - Partners, endorsements

5. TEAM
   - Why you are the right team
   - Relevant experience
   - Local knowledge advantage

6. BUDGET & USE OF FUNDS
   - Detailed breakdown
   - Cost per beneficiary
   - Leverage (other funding attracted)

7. SUSTAINABILITY
   - How will you survive after the grant?
   - Revenue model
   - Path to self-sufficiency

8. IMPACT MEASUREMENT
   - Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)
   - How you'll measure them
   - Reporting frequency
```

## Winning Narrative Frameworks

### The "Local Expert" Angle
"As a Sierra Leonean entrepreneur building for Sierra Leonean farmers, I understand challenges that external consultants cannot. [Specific local insight]. This insider knowledge is our competitive advantage."

### The "Technology + Impact" Angle
"Our platform isn't just a marketplace — it's economic infrastructure. Every transaction creates data that helps farmers access credit, enables better agricultural planning, and reduces food insecurity."

### The Scale Narrative
"Sierra Leone is our validation market. The same model applies to Guinea, Liberia, and across West Africa — a $2.4B opportunity serving 15M smallholder farmers."

## Impact Metrics That Funders Love
- Number of farmers registered / served
- % increase in farmer income
- Tons of food waste reduced
- # of jobs created (direct + indirect)
- $ volume of transactions facilitated
- # women farmers reached
- % of transactions via mobile money (financial inclusion)

Always frame your ask around ROI for the funder. A $50,000 grant that creates $500,000 in farmer income = 10x social ROI.
