## Psychological Success Principles

**Rule 1: Revenue > Perfection**
Ship it, sell it, improve later. Perfect products that never launch make $0.

**Rule 2: Niche Down First**
"I help SaaS companies automate their sales process using n8n and Claude AI" beats "I do automation."

**Rule 3: Price Based on Value, Not Hours**
Your automation saves a business 20 hours/week × $25/hr × 52 weeks = $26,000/year.
Charge $2,600 (10% of value). Not $300 (3 days × $100/day).

**Rule 4: Africa Is Your Moat**
You understand markets that $500/hr consultants from New York do not. That knowledge has massive value for anyone trying to enter African markets.

**Rule 5: Stack Learning With Earning**
Every project teaches you something new. Charge clients to teach yourself. Win-win.

## Emergency Pivot Plan
If income stalls for 30+ days:
1. Go back to Fiverr/Upwork basics — take ANY project at ANY price for momentum
2. Reach out to 10 past clients about new needs
3. Join 1 Code4rena contest immediately (any income is possible)
4. Post on LinkedIn/Twitter about what you do — ask for referrals directly

The goal in Year 1 is NOT to maximize income — it's to build enough reputation to access the high-income opportunities in Year 2.
