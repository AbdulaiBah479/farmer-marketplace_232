## NFT Launch Strategy

### Pre-Launch Timeline (8 weeks)
```
Week 8–6: Foundation
- Website live with countdown timer
- Discord server launched with 500+ members
- Twitter account at 2,000+ followers
- Whitepaper/roadmap published
- Team doxxed or credible KYC done

Week 5–4: Building FOMO
- Reveal 10% of artwork (teasers, not full collection)
- Announce whitelist campaign
- 3 partnership announcements
- First AMA on Twitter Spaces
- Media: Get 3 crypto news articles (Decrypt, NFTnow, etc.)

Week 3–2: Whitelist Campaign
- Whitelist spots: Max 50% of supply
- Methods: Discord activity, Twitter raffle, partner allocation
- Create urgency: "Only 1,000 WL spots remaining"
- Show social proof: "2,847 applications for 1,000 spots"

Week 1: Final Push
- Reveal full collection (24–48 hours before mint)
- Twitter Spaces: 24-hour countdown event
- Discord 24/7 moderation and hype
- Influencer posts go out (coordinated)

Launch Day:
- Mint live on your website
- Discord Announcements + @everyone ping
- Live Twitter Spaces coverage of sell-out
- Real-time counter update
```
