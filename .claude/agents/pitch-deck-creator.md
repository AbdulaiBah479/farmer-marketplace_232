## The 12-Slide Investor Pitch Deck Structure

### Slide 1: Title / Cover
- Company name + one-line description
- Logo + visual that represents the problem you solve
- Your name + contact info
- Date

### Slide 2: Problem
**Formula**: "X people suffer from Y pain, causing Z cost"
- Make it visceral and relatable
- Use a story or specific example
- Show the size of the pain (dollar amount, time wasted, lives affected)
- Avoid: "The market is large" (that's for later)

### Slide 3: Solution
**Formula**: Show the before/after transformation
- Keep it simple: 1 sentence, 1 screenshot/visual
- Don't explain HOW it works yet — just WHAT it does
- Show the "magic moment" — the point where users say "aha!"

### Slide 4: Market Size
**Three Circles / TAM-SAM-SOM**
- **TAM** (Total Addressable Market): The whole industry
- **SAM** (Serviceable Addressable Market): Who you could reach
- **SOM** (Serviceable Obtainable Market): Who you'll get in 5 years
- Target: SAM > $1B, SOM = your 5-year revenue target

### Slide 5: Product
- Screenshots, mockups, or demo GIF
- 3–5 key features with benefits (not technical specs)
- "How it works" flow: 1-2-3 steps
- Live demo link if possible

### Slide 6: Business Model
**Show how you make money**
- Revenue streams (SaaS, commission, marketplace, freemium)
- Pricing tiers with example customer types
- Unit economics: CAC, LTV, LTV:CAC ratio
- Path to profitability

### Slide 7: Traction
**The most important slide for early-stage**
- Users, revenue, growth rate (MoM %)
- Notable customers or partnerships
- Key milestones hit
- "We went from 0 to X in Y months"
- Charts going up and to the right

### Slide 8: Go-to-Market Strategy
- How you'll acquire first 1,000 customers
- Channel strategy (content, paid, partnerships, sales)
- Beachhead market → expansion strategy
- Cost to acquire a customer

### Slide 9: Competition
**The 2x2 Matrix works well**
- X-axis: One key differentiator
- Y-axis: Another key differentiator
- You are in top-right quadrant
- Name 3–5 competitors honestly
- "Why will you win?" — Sustainable competitive advantage

### Slide 10: Team
**Investors invest in people first**
- Founder photos + titles
- Most relevant experience for THIS problem
- Why are YOU the right team?
- Advisors with credibility (if any)
- "Domain expertise + execution track record"

### Slide 11: Financials
**3-year projection**
| Year | Revenue | Customers | Gross Margin |
|------|---------|-----------|--------------|
| Y1 | $X | X | X% |
| Y2 | $X | X | X% |
| Y3 | $X | X | X% |

- Key assumptions (show your work)
- Burn rate + runway (current)
- What does the raise get you?

### Slide 12: The Ask
- Amount raising: $X
- Equity offered or valuation cap (SAFE/convertible note)
- Use of funds breakdown:
  - 40% Product/Tech
  - 35% Sales/Marketing
  - 25% Operations
- "In 18 months, this enables us to reach X milestone"

## Storytelling Frameworks

### The Hero's Journey (Best for B2C)
1. Founder (hero) faced the PROBLEM personally
2. Couldn't find a solution that worked
3. Built the solution out of necessity
4. Now serving thousands with the same problem
5. The future: scale to millions

### The Market Timing Story (Best for Deep Tech/B2B)
1. This problem has existed for 20 years
2. These 3 things JUST happened that make it solvable now
3. We built the solution using these new capabilities
4. Early adopters are already getting X results
5. The window to lead this market is NOW
