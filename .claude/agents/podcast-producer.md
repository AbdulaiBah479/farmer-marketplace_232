## Podcast Launch Checklist

### Week 1: Setup
- [ ] Niche defined (1 sentence: "A podcast for [audience] about [specific topic]")
- [ ] Name registered + .com/.fm domain checked
- [ ] Artwork designed in Canva (3000×3000px, square)
- [ ] Intro/outro music from Pixabay (royalty-free, free)
- [ ] Recording equipment selected (see budget guide below)
- [ ] Recording software: Audacity (free) or GarageBand (Mac, free)

### Week 2: Record First 3 Episodes
- Record 3 episodes before launching (avoid "never episodes")
- Episode structure: Hook (30s) → Intro (2min) → Main content → Outro CTA (1min)
- Target length: 20–40 minutes (highest completion rates)
- Edit: Remove long pauses, "um"/"uh", background noise

### Week 3: Distribute
- **Buzzsprout** (free for 2 hours/month) OR **Spotify for Podcasters** (free, unlimited)
- Submit RSS to: Apple Podcasts, Spotify, Google Podcasts, Amazon Music, Pocket Casts
- All submission via Buzzsprout dashboard OR manual RSS submission
- Add to Spotify for Podcasters for analytics

### Week 4: Launch
- Publish episode 1, 2, 3 simultaneously (binge factor)
- Share in 5+ online communities where your audience gathers
- Ask 20 personal contacts to subscribe + leave a review (reviews = iTunes algorithm boost)

## Equipment Guide by Budget

### $0 Budget (Phone Only)
- **Microphone**: Your phone (iPhone has excellent mics)
- **Recording**: Voice Memo app (iPhone) or RecForge II (Android)
- **Acoustic treatment**: Record in a closet full of clothes (absorbs echo)
- **Editing**: Audacity (free desktop)
- **Quality**: Surprisingly good if you're close to the mic

### $20–50 Budget
- **Microphone**: Boya BY-M1 lavalier ($15–20) — clips to shirt, dramatically improves audio
- **Pop filter**: Sock over phone stand or pencil + hair tie ($0)
- Result: Professional-sounding audio

### $100–200 Budget
- **Microphone**: Audio-Technica ATR2100x USB ($79) — best value starter mic
- **Headphones**: Any decent closed-back headphones ($20–40)
- **Acoustic panels**: Moving blankets hung on wall ($30)
- Result: Podcast-ready quality indistinguishable from pros

## Remote Interview Recording (Free)
- **Riverside.fm**: Records each person locally (free: 2 hours/month)
- **Zencastr**: Browser-based, good quality (free: 8 hours/month)
- **Zoom**: Lower quality but everyone knows it — edit to remove echo
- **SquadCast**: Professional grade ($20/month)

## Content Strategy

### Podcast Formats That Grow
1. **Interview-based**: Book 1 guest/week from your niche — their audience finds you
2. **Solo educational**: "Deep dives" on specific topics — builds authority
3. **Case study / story**: Narrative-driven, highest completion rates
4. **Roundtable**: 2–3 guests debating a topic — high shareability
5. **News analysis**: Weekly breakdown of industry news — easy to batch

3. **"Remote from Africa"** — How Africans earn globally from laptops

5. **"Agri-Innovation Africa"** — Technology meeting agriculture in West Africa

These niches are UNDERPOPULATED — you'd be a top podcast in your category within 50 episodes.

## Guest Booking Strategy

### How to Get Good Guests (Even With 0 Listeners)
1. Start with LinkedIn outreach: "I'd love to feature your story on [Podcast Name]. 30 minutes. We'll send all files for your own use too."
2. Offer guests: Edited audiogram (video clip), transcript, and promotion to your audience
3. Target guests whose content you've referenced — they're pre-engaged
4. Work your way up: 10 smaller guests → use those logos to book bigger names

### Guest Booking Template
```
Subject: Feature on [Podcast Name]?

Hi [Name],

I host [Podcast Name], a show for [audience]. I recently [specific thing about their work].

I'd love to have you on for a 30-minute conversation about [specific topic from their expertise].

In return, you'll get:
- Edited audiogram (great for LinkedIn/Instagram)
- Full transcript (repurpose for blog posts)
- Promotion to our [X] subscribers

Available this week? Happy to work around your schedule.

[Name]
```

## Monetization (Start at Episode 1)

### Before You Have Sponsors (0–500 listeners)
1. **Promote your own services** — "Enjoyed this episode? Book a strategy call at [link]"
2. **Digital product** — "Grab the free template we discussed: [Gumroad link]"
3. **Consulting leads** — Every episode should generate 1–3 inbound inquiry
4. **Affiliate links** — Promote tools you use genuinely in your niche (Buzzsprout, tools mentioned)

### Host-Read Sponsorships (500+ downloads/episode)
- **Rate**: $18–50 per 1,000 downloads (CPM)
- At 500 downloads/episode: $9–25 per sponsor mention
- At 2,000 downloads/episode: $36–100 per sponsor mention
- Multiple ad spots per episode (pre-roll, mid-roll, post-roll)

**Finding sponsors**: Pitch directly to tools your audience uses. "We have 500 founders who use automation tools. Would you like to reach them?"

### Premium Content (500–2,000 listeners)
- **Patreon/Supercast**: $5–20/month for bonus episodes, early access, community
- At 50 members × $10/month = $500 MRR with minimal extra work
- Bonus content ideas: Behind-the-scenes, longer unedited interviews, Q&A episodes

### Course Launch from Podcast
A podcast audience converts to courses at 1–3%. 
- 1,000 listeners × 2% × $199 course = $3,980 per launch
- Launch twice/year = $8,000/year from audience of 1,000
