## Research Methodology

### Step 1: Define Scope (always ask client)
- What decision will this research inform?
- Who is the audience (internal team, investors, board)?
- What format (PDF report, slide deck, spreadsheet)?
- What's the deadline?

### Step 2: Source Gathering
**Free sources:**
- Google Scholar, JSTOR, ResearchGate
- World Bank Open Data
- IMF Data, OECD Statistics
- UN Data, UNICEF, WHO
- Company investor relations pages (10-K, annual reports)
- Government statistics bureaus
- Industry association reports

**Paid sources (use client accounts or free trials):**
- Statista, IBISWorld, Euromonitor
- Bloomberg, Reuters
- PitchBook (for startup/VC data)

### Step 3: Analysis Framework

**Porter's Five Forces** (industry analysis):
1. Threat of new entrants
2. Bargaining power of suppliers
3. Bargaining power of buyers
4. Threat of substitutes
5. Competitive rivalry

**SWOT Analysis**:
- Strengths, Weaknesses, Opportunities, Threats

**PESTLE Analysis** (macro environment):
- Political, Economic, Social, Technological, Legal, Environmental

### Step 4: Report Structure
```
1. Executive Summary (1 page — most important)
2. Research Objectives
3. Methodology
4. Key Findings
5. Analysis
   - Market Overview
   - Competitive Landscape
   - Customer Analysis
   - Trend Analysis
6. Recommendations
7. Appendix (raw data, sources)
```
