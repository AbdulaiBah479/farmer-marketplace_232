## The SaaS Growth Framework (AARRR)

### Acquisition: Getting Your First Users

#### Channel Playbook by Stage
| Stage | Best Channel | Why |
|-------|-------------|-----|
| 0→100 users | Direct outreach + communities | Fast, free, feedback-rich |
| 100→1K | Content SEO + Product Hunt | Compound growth, low cost |
| 1K→10K | Paid ads + partnerships | Scale what's working |
| 10K+ | Virality + referral programs | Leverage existing users |

#### Product Hunt Launch Strategy
```
1. 3 weeks before: Build in public on Twitter/LinkedIn
2. 2 weeks before: Grow hunter network (find people with large followings)
3. 1 week before: Email your waitlist to "mark their calendar"
4. Launch day: Post at 12:01am PST (start of voting period)
5. Launch day: Personally DM first 200 supporters
6. Result: Top 5 of the day = 500–2,000 signups
```

### Activation: Converting Signups to Active Users

#### The "First Value Moment" Framework
Every SaaS has a magic moment — the first time a user gets real value.
- **Notion**: First page created
- **Slack**: First message sent
- **Shopify**: First product added
- **Your tool**: What is YOUR magic moment?

Optimize onboarding to get users to that magic moment as fast as possible.

#### Activation Optimization Checklist
- [ ] Signup flow < 5 steps (every extra step = 10% drop-off)
- [ ] Welcome email arrives within 60 seconds
- [ ] In-app checklist guides to first success (like HubSpot's setup guide)
- [ ] One clear call-to-action on dashboard (not 10 choices)
- [ ] Quick-start video < 3 minutes
- [ ] Live chat available during first login

### Retention: Keeping Users Active

#### Churn Analysis Framework
```
Why users churn:
1. Never got value (activation failure) → Fix: Better onboarding
2. Got value but don't need it anymore → Fix: Use case expansion
3. Found a better alternative → Fix: Feature parity + positioning
4. Too expensive → Fix: Pricing tiers or annual discount
5. Company went out of business → Fix: Target healthier segment

Track: 
- 7-day retention rate (% of signups active after 7 days)
- 30-day retention rate (% still active after 30 days)
- Net Revenue Retention (NRR): (revenue end of month / start) × 100
  - Good: >100% (you're growing from existing customers)
  - Bad: <90% (churn is outpacing growth)
```

#### Reducing Churn Playbook
1. **Email campaigns at churn signals** (no login in 7 days → "Are you stuck?")
2. **In-app tooltips** for unused features with high retention correlation
3. **Quarterly Business Reviews** for annual enterprise customers
4. **Pause instead of cancel** option (reduces cancellations 20–30%)
5. **Exit survey** (mandatory — data on why they leave)
