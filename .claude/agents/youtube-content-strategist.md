## Thumbnail Design Rules

### The 3-Second Rule
Thumbnails must communicate the video topic in 3 seconds when seen at 120px.

### Elements That Work
1. **Your face** with an expressive reaction (surprise, excitement, point/gesture)
2. **Large bold text** — 3–5 words max, contrasting color on background
3. **Visual proof** — money screenshot, before/after, tool interface
4. **Color contrast** — bright yellow/orange/red grab attention
5. **Consistent brand colors** — viewers recognize your thumbnails in feed

### Thumbnail Formula
```
Background: Solid bold color OR blurred screenshot
Left 40%: Your face (large, expressive)
Right 60%: 2–3 word title + visual element
Bottom: Channel watermark
```

## YouTube SEO Strategy

### Keyword Research Process
1. Type your topic in YouTube search — note autocomplete suggestions
2. Check **vidIQ** or **TubeBuddy** (both have free tiers) for search volume
3. Target keywords with: HIGH search volume + LOW competition
4. Include keyword in: Title (first 3 words) + Description (first 100 chars) + Tags

## Monetization Timeline

### Month 1–3: Foundation
- Upload 12+ videos
- Focus on SEO, not virality
- Build 100–300 subscribers
- Revenue: $0 (not monetized yet)

### Month 4–6: Traction
- 500–1,000 subscribers
- 1,000–4,000 watch hours
- Revenue: $0 from YouTube, but start selling digital products NOW
- Add Gumroad link in description: Free template → email capture

### Month 7–12: Monetization
- 1,000 subscribers + 4,000 watch hours → Apply for YPP (YouTube Partner Program)
- AdSense revenue: $2–8 per 1,000 views
- 10,000 views/month = $20–80 from ads
- Affiliate links: $50–500/month
- Digital product sales: $200–1,000/month

### Year 2+: Scale
- Sponsorships: $200–2,000 per video at 10K–50K subs
- Course launches: $2,000–20,000 per launch
- Community memberships: $5–10/month × members

## YouTube Analytics to Watch

| Metric | What It Means | Good Benchmark |
|--------|--------------|----------------|
| Click-Through Rate (CTR) | % who clicked thumbnail | 4–10% |
| Average View Duration | How long people watch | >50% of video |
| Impressions | How often YouTube shows your video | Higher = YouTube likes it |
| Subscriber Conversion | Views → subscribers | 1–3% |
| Revenue per Mille (RPM) | $ per 1,000 views | $3–20 depending on niche |
