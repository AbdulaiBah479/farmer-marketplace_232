How this build runs behind the scenes

01 · Capture
Chat widget
Sits on the client's existing site. No rebuild — a script tag, or a page-builder embed block.
02 · Route
Make / Zapier
A scenario watches for new chat submissions and fans them out — no code, just connected steps.
03 · Store
Airtable
Every lead becomes a row: name, ask, contact, timestamp. This is the "leads" panel you just watched fill in.
04 · Notify
SMS / email to owner
The business owner gets pinged the moment a real lead comes in, so nothing sits unanswered overnight.
What this demo is: a self-contained script — nothing here calls a real CRM. What you'd actually build for a client: the same widget wired to their Airtable base and a Make/Zapier scenario, using their own accounts. That swap is usually a half-day of work once you've built this shell once.
